How to Play

  1. You start with a 4-letter word (e.g. FORK).
  2. Your goal is to reach the target word (e.g. TUCK).
  3. Click a letter to change it. Only one letter can change at a time.
  4. Each step must be a valid English word.
  5. Try to reach the target in as few moves as possible!
